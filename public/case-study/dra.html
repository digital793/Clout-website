The Glow Up Behind DRA Homes' Digital Presence: From 7K to 116K

Chennai has always had a lot going for it. The food, the culture, the history, the people. But for years, it lost the conversation to Bangalore and Mumbai cities that had somehow claimed the narrative of being young, exciting, and worth visiting. Nobody said "let's go to Chennai".

Meet DRA Homes

DRA Homes is one of Chennai's most established real estate developers, with a legacy that spans over 40 years. They have built a reputation around trust, transparency and timely delivery, earning the confidence of thousands of families across the city. With a strong portfolio of luxury apartments, villas and plots, DRA Homes has always stood for quality construction and long term value.
When Cloutgency partnered with DRA Homes in May 2025, the goal was clear. Take a brand with decades of credibility and give it a digital presence that feels just as premium, present and alive as the homes they build.

The Space DRA Homes Competes In

Chennai's real estate market is home to some strong, well established names, including Amara Homes, Casagrand and CeeBros. As one of the top builders in the city, DRA Homes has always aimed to compete with the very best, not just in construction quality, but in how the brand shows up online.
Real estate as a category often gets a reputation for being a little predictable on social media. DRA Homes wanted to break that mold and build a digital identity that felt fresh, aspirational and genuinely engaging.

Where Cloutgency Came In

Cloutgency has been DRA Homes' digital partner for over a year now, handling a full range of work across platforms:

  • Instagram growth campaigns and daily content
  • YouTube channel setup and growth
  • LinkedIn presence and content strategy
  • High quality project shoots and photography
  • Campaign concept development, including festive and thematic campaigns
  • A complete luxury rebrand for their iHeart project launch
  • Monthly content calendars
  • AEO, helping DRA Homes show up when people ask AI tools about builders in Chennai
This has been a true long term partnership, built on consistency, creativity and a shared goal of helping DRA Homes stand out.

What Clout did

Branding: Giving DRA Homes a Luxury Identity

This is where Cloutgency's work truly stood out.
When DRA Homes launched their iHeart project, they wanted the entire experience to feel luxurious from the very first impression. Cloutgency led a complete luxury rebrand of their social media presence to bring that vision to life.

Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn: Building Presence Across the Board

Cloutgency's work with DRA Homes has never been limited to just one platform.

Instagram

On Instagram, consistent content, high quality project shoots and follower campaigns helped grow their community from 7,000 to 55,000 followers.

Youtube

On YouTube, the channel grew from 800 to an impressive 55,000 subscribers in just two to three months, a fast and meaningful jump that reflects how well the content resonated once it found its footing. Now reaching an 80 fold 1,66,000 subscribers.

linkedin

On LinkedIn, growth has been steady and intentional, moving from 8,000 to 11,000 followers. LinkedIn tends to be a slower, more thoughtful platform, and DRA Homes' presence there continues to build strong professional credibility.
Alongside platform growth, Cloutgency also developed timely, engaging campaigns, including a World Cup themed activation, keeping DRA Homes' content calendar fresh, relevant and connected to what audiences were already talking about.

What This Project Taught Us

Working with DRA Homes has been a genuine pleasure for the Cloutgency team. Real estate is often seen as a traditional, cautious industry, but DRA Homes has consistently shown a willingness to innovate, experiment and embrace new ideas. That openness, paired with daily communication between our teams, has made this one of our smoothest and most rewarding long term partnerships.
It is a reminder that even in a legacy driven industry like real estate, there is always room to grow, glow up and connect with people in fresh new ways.
