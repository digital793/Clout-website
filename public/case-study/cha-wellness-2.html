"Super Chennai. It's Hot. It's Happening”

How Cloutgency Built Chennai's Loudest Digital Voice From Zero to 1.6 Million

Chennai has always had a lot going for it. The food, the culture, the history, the people. But for years, it lost the conversation to Bangalore and Mumbai cities that had somehow claimed the narrative of being young, exciting, and worth visiting. Nobody said "let's go to Chennai".

Super Chennai was built to change that.

Backed by Ranjit Ratud (MD of DRA Homes) and CEO Karthik, and developed in collaboration with Blue Noodles and Ananthora, Super Chennai launched in 2023 as a public social initiative not a brand, not a media company, but a movement. Its mission is to showcase Chennai in a different, better light. To give Gen Z and young Indians a reason to feel proud of the city they live in, and to make those outside it genuinely curious.

The tagline said it all: “Super Chennai. It's Hot. It's Happening”

The platform operates across entertainment, F&B, events, and culture creating content that travels across Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook. Vox pops on city streets, Restaurant and eatery features, Event coverages, Cultural spotlights. Conversations with Chennai's most prominent names. And a structured sponsorship model where brands, retailers, and businesses can reach Chennai's audience through Super Chennai's growing community.
Beyond content, Super Chennai carries a larger purpose driving employment, boosting the city's economy, and increasing interest in Chennai as a place to live, invest, and build in.

Brand's Requirements

When Super Chennai came to Cloutgency, the idea existed but the platform didn't. There was no community, no content engine, no social presence, and no proof that Chennai had an audience hungry for this kind of storytelling.

The core requirements were:

  • Build a social media presence from absolute zero
  • Create a consistent content identity that felt native to Gen Z without alienating older audiences
  • Develop content formats that could showcase Chennai across food, events, culture, and people
  • Build a community not just a following
  • Execute campaigns and activations that would put Super Chennai on the map as Chennai's defining digital voice
Nobody had done this for Chennai before. There was no blueprint to follow.

Competitor ANalysis

Super Chennai's closest competitors in the space were pages like New to Chennai and other city-based community pages operating in the Chennai digital space.
But the gap was clear: most Chennai focused pages were functional and informational, they told you what was happening, not why Chennai mattered. They lacked identity, energy, and the kind of storytelling that makes a city feel alive.
Super Chennai wasn't built to list events. It was built to make Chennai feel like a personality one that Gen Z could relate to, share, and be proud of. That emotional positioning was the whitespace no competitor had claimed, and that became the creative foundation for everything Cloutgency built.

What Clout did

Community Building From Zero

We built Super Chennai's entire social media community from scratch across Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook. No inherited audience, no paid shortcuts. Pure content strategy, creative execution, and community momentum. The result was 1.6 million followers and counting.

CAMPaigns

The Car Rally Campaign

One of Super Chennai's most memorable activities was a city wide car rally that began at Lady Andal School and wound through Chennai before culminating at VGP on ECR. The campaign captured the scale and spirit of Chennai across different pockets of the city, and gave Super Chennai a physical, real world presence to complement its digital one.

Arattai Sessions

A recurring intimate format, small gatherings of 50 to 60 people brought together to hear prominent Chennai personalities speak about how the city shaped them, launched their careers, or built their identity. Part conversation, part community event, part content these sessions reinforced Super Chennai's positioning as a platform that celebrated people who chose Chennai.

Made in Chennai Podcast

A long form podcast hosted by Sidhanth and published on YouTube giving Super Chennai a voice in the growing podcast space while putting Chennai's stories on a global platform.

Icon of the Month

A monthly award initiative recognising a prominent Chennai personality, a business owner, creator, or community builder who had done meaningful work for the city. This format gave Super Chennai a recurring content anchor while celebrating the very people who make Chennai worth talking about.

Sponsorship & Monetisation Model

We helped structure Super Chennai's commercial offering a tiered plan where brands could invest in reels, platform features, and website listings turning the community we built into a viable, scalable media business.
